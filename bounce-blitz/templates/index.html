Email Verification Statuses & Recommendations

Status Score What it means What to do
Safe 100 Mailbox exists and accepts mail; no risk flags. Send with confidence.
Role 70 Valid, but a shared company address (info@, support@). Fine for B2B outreach; usually skip for newsletters.
Catch-All 50 The domain accepts mail for any name, so mailbox cannot be confirmed. Send with caution, as a separate segment.
Disposable 0 A throwaway address that expires shortly after creation. Do not send; block it at sign-up.
Inbox Full 35 A real mailbox that cannot accept new mail right now. Skip for now and re-verify later.
Spamtrap 0 An address that exists only to catch careless senders. Never send.
Disabled 0 Existed once; the provider has shut it down. Do not send.
Invalid 0 No mailbox exists: a guaranteed hard bounce. Do not send.
Unknown 15 The server would not give a definitive answer. Not charged; re-verify later.
